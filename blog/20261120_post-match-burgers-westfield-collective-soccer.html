Post-Match Burgers at Westfield Collective During This Weekend's Soccer Showcase

The November 20–22 women’s college soccer showcase brings teams, coaches, and families to Westfield, just one mile north of Westfield Collective.

After hours on the touchline or between showcase matches, stop by Smash & Grab Burgers inside Westfield Collective (750 E. 181st St.) for thin-smashed patties with crispy, caramelized edges, melted cheese on a toasted bun, golden fries, and house-sauced wings.

Weekend Hours at Westfield Collective

  • Friday: 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM
  • Saturday: 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM
  • Sunday: 10:00 AM – 9:00 PM

Smash & Grab Burgers is an independent restaurant concept at Westfield Collective and is not affiliated with or sponsored by Droplight Grand Park Sports Campus or the showcase organizers.

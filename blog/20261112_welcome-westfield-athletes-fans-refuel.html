Welcome to Westfield, Athletes & Fans! Refuel at Smash & Grab Burgers This Weekend

Heading to Westfield this weekend for the Grand Park Men’s College Showcase (November 13–15)? Whether you’re competing on the pitch, coaching from the sideline, or cheering with the family, Smash & Grab Burgers is right down the road from Droplight Grand Park Sports Campus and ready to welcome you.

Located just minutes away inside the Westfield Collective (750 E 181st St), we’re firing up the griddle and smashing fresh, juicy burgers built for hungry athletes and tournament crowds.

Why Teams & Families Love Stopping By

  • Simple Food, Smashed Right: Every patty is pressed thin on a screaming-hot griddle for those signature crispy, lacy edges and a juicy center—layered with melted cheese on a soft, toasted bun.
